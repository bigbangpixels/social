Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Big Bang Pixels is a small design shop based in Switzerland. We sell embroidered and printed products through our Etsy shop. This policy explains what this website and the Big Bang Pixels TikTok app do with personal data.

This website

social.bigbangpixels.store is a static page with links to our shop and social accounts. It has no account system, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

The Big Bang Pixels TikTok app

We use our own TikTok developer app only to publish our own posts (photo carousels of our products) to our own TikTok account, @bigbangpixels. The app:

  • is authorized only by the Big Bang Pixels account;
  • receives from TikTok the access tokens and basic profile information of that account, which we store privately and use only to upload our posts;
  • does not collect, store or share any data about other TikTok users, viewers or customers;
  • never sells or transfers data to anyone.

The pictures sent to TikTok are hosted on this website so TikTok can fetch them.

Orders

Purchases happen on Etsy. Etsy's privacy policy applies to your order, payment and shipping data. Orders are produced by our print-on-demand partner, which receives only the data needed to make and ship your item.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, through the "Message" button on our Etsy shop. We answer within 30 days.

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
